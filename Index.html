Research directions

Three fields. One research initiative.

Different scientific questions connected by a common commitment to modelling, evidence and open knowledge.

01 / BIOPHYSICS

Origin of Life

Computational modelling of protocell systems, catalytic interactions, resource availability and the emergence of evolutionary dynamics.

02 / PHYSICS & PHILOSOPHY

Foundations of Quantum Mechanics

Research on ensemble interpretations, D. I. Blokhintsev, quantum decoherence and the problem of measurement.

03 / PALEOBIOLOGY

PaleoSound

Evidence-based computational approaches to reconstructing sounds of extinct organisms from fossils and scientific literature.

About the initiative

Independent work. Open results.

This is an independent research initiative based in the Republic of Moldova. Its purpose is to develop research projects, publish findings, share computational tools and make scientific materials openly accessible.

The initiative is currently developed independently and is not affiliated with a university or research institution.
