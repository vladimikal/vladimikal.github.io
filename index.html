Research programme

Three directions

Different objects of study, different methods. A shared interest in how complex structures emerge, persist and change.

01 / Origin of Life

Protocell Dynamics

Computational modelling of protocell populations, catalysis, resource availability and the emergence of evolutionary dynamics.

Focus: minimal systems, inheritance, population behaviour.

02 / Quantum Foundations

Quantum Measurement

Historical and conceptual research on ensemble interpretations, D. I. Blokhintsev, decoherence and the problem of quantum measurement.

Focus: interpretation, physical theory, history of ideas.

03 / Computational Paleobiology

PaleoSound

Evidence-based approaches to reconstructing possible sounds of extinct organisms using fossil anatomy and scientific literature.

Focus: fossil evidence, acoustic models, uncertainty.
